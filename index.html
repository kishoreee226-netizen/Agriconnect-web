PRIVACY POLICY FOR AGRICONNECT

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to AgriConnect ("we," "our," or "us"). AgriConnect is owned and operated by AgriConnect Technologies (UDYAM Registration: UDYAM-TS-31-0063048). We are dedicated to respecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, use, and disclosure when you use the AgriConnect mobile application and related web services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identity Information: Name, phone number, and primary role (Farmer, Agricultural Laborer, or Trader/Buyer) collected during profile onboarding and authentication.
  • Hyperlocal Location Information: Approximate or precise GPS location collected to connect farmers with local laborers, nearby machinery operators, and relevant regional market yards (e.g., Warangal Enamamula Market).
  • Agricultural & Listing Data: Crop listings, produce quantities, expected pricing, crop images, machinery types, and availability schedules uploaded voluntarily by users.
  • Device & Diagnostic Logs: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, crash logs, and performance metrics to ensure app stability and user support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable hyperlocal matching between farmers, laborers, and buyers.
  • To display location-specific mandi/market yard price estimates.
  • To authenticate user sessions via secure OTP mechanisms.
  • To facilitate voice assistance and accessibility features in local languages.
  • To enforce our terms, prevent fraudulent activity, and comply with applicable laws.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Location: Used exclusively to find nearby agricultural workers, machinery, and market pricing within your district.
  • Microphone: Used solely when voice-search or accessibility speech-to-text features are actively engaged.
  • Storage / Media: Required only for uploading crop photos or equipment images for sales listings.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared strictly under the following conditions:

  • Between Platform Users: Contact details and crop listings are visible to prospective buyers, workers, or farmers to facilitate agricultural engagement.
  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (such as Google Cloud/Firebase) and SMS gateway providers for OTP verification.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by Indian law, judicial decree, or law enforcement authorities.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain user information for as long as your account remains active. You possess the right to delete your account and associated profile data at any time. To request complete data erasure, please email us directly with your registered phone number at agriconnect.office@gmail.com. Your records will be permanently removed within 30 days of confirmation.

6. Children's Privacy

AgriConnect does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 18. If we discover that personal information has been collected from a minor, it will be promptly removed.
